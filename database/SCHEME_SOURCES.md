## Important limitations
- PMFBY must be checked against the applicable Karnataka district/crop/season notification. Do not hardcode a generic deadline, premium or sum insured.
- SSP is a directory/portal for multiple schemes, not one universal scholarship. Each department's current scheme notice must be researched separately.
- PM-USP CSSS is included only as a discovery record. Its current scheme specification must be reviewed before encoding eligibility or benefit amounts.
- PM-KISAN's broad eligibility and exclusions need full rule transcription and Karnataka land-record process confirmation before automated pass/fail decisions.
